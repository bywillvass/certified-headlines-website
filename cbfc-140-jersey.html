CBFC 140 Year
Jersey Project

To celebrate Canterbury Bankstown FC's historic 140th Anniversary, the club released a limited-edition commemorative jersey — a tribute to the legacy and community that has defined the club for over a century.

Certified Headlines was brought in to deliver a full photoshoot of the jersey for marketing and promotional use, capturing the kit in a way that honoured the milestone. We also provided coverage of the club's media engagements, attending and documenting their interviews with multiple news outlets to ensure the announcement reached audiences far and wide.
